Bigger isn't always better: if the bars are too wide overall, some scanners (especially ones tuned for compact retail barcodes) can't fit the whole thing in one sweep/frame and never lock on, even though each bar is easy to resolve. What the result tells you

  1. Nothing scans, at any size: scanner isn't reading anything from this screen/setup - check it's powered on, connected, in "keyboard wedge" mode, and try scanning a barcode on any product box to confirm it works at all.
  2. QR scans but no Code128 size does: scanner is likely set to QR-only, or isn't in the right symbology mode for 1D codes - check the scanner's configuration/manual for enabling Code128.
  3. One or more of the smaller Code128 sizes scan, but the biggest ones don't: confirms your scanner's field of view can't cover an overly wide barcode - note the label (e.g. "Size C") of the smallest/largest ones that worked and tell me, so the certificate barcodes get sized to match.
  4. All sizes scan here but the certificate page still fails: that's a caching issue - hard refresh the certificate page (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R), or open it in a private/incognito window, so the browser isn't running an old cached script.
